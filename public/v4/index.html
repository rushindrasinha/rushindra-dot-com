01 The through line

A FEW DIFFERENT LIVES.
THE SAME PERSON.

I started with games, qualified as a doctor, and kept coming back to building things. Here is how those paths joined up.

THE INSTINCT

PLAY.
LEARN.
BUILD.
REPEAT.

I taught myself to code during medical school. The games were never just a distraction. They were my first lesson in running something people chose to come back to.

The longer biography ↗
  1. 2001

    The first LAN.

    Counter-Strike. Then years of Dota and competitive games.

  2. 2003

    World Cyber Games.

    Competed at WCG. Long before founding an esports organisation, I was a player.

  3. 2008

    Built my own online world.

    Ran Phoenix RO, a Ragnarok Online server. Sold it in 2009, at 18. My first exit.

  4. 2014

    Medicine, code and a camera.

    MBBS at D.Y. Patil Medical College. Live-streamed surgeries using Google Glass. With Mohit Israney, first place at Tribeca and second in Beijing.

  5. 2014

    The doctor in the hacker house.

    Moved to San Francisco. Ended up in WIRED’s “No Exit”. A very different kind of education.

  6. 2015

    A startup in Los Angeles.

    Built and exited Hashtack, a social media startup for events and brands.

  7. 2017

    Stanford. Then home.

    Executive education at Stanford GSB × School of Medicine: The Innovative Health Care Leader. Came home and founded Global Esports in August, with Mohit as co-founder.

  8. 2025

    AI becomes the building material.

    The audited usage starts in August. In January 2026, I started Ares on OpenClaw. An AI operating system that runs from Discord.

  9. 2026

    A trophy. And the next chapter.

    Global Esports won VCT Pacific Stage 2, played Masters London and its first Champions in Shanghai. Launched Jeufy. Still building Ares, Aarees and Verticals.gg.
